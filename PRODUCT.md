# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Users
Anyone who wants to get to know Sound of the Future. The user explicitly prioritizes easy navigation for everyone, rather than a single conversion audience.
## Product Purpose
Introduce the youth-led hearing-health nonprofit, explain its work, show accomplishments, and help visitors join, donate, listen, or make contact.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Existing React/Vite website with seven routes, hosted on Netlify. Preserve founder biographies and mission. Use the user's actual photos, not generated imagery. Public-facing links come from the existing website and public Linktree.
## Evidence on Hand
User-supplied accomplishment document, updated October 4, 2026; Photos for Marketing Drive folder; existing founder portraits and logo. Achievement totals are organization-reported. Do not invent impact calculations or sponsor contributions.
## Product Principles
Make the organization easy to understand; keep navigation available on small screens; show actual work; use specific, factual language.
