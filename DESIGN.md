# Design System: Sound of the Future
## Overview
Keep the recognizable blue, navy, white, and original logo. Bring the organization’s real community photographs into the foreground. Direct language and visible navigation make the site welcoming to any visitor.
## Colors
Blue identifies actions. Navy anchors typography and impact sections. White and light blue separate content without decorative gradients.
## Typography
Use the existing Inter body face with system fallbacks. Headings are bold, compact, balanced, and never tighter than -0.04em. Body text uses generous line height.
## Layout
A centered, flexible content width; large documentary images; clear sections with varied density. Desktop navigation becomes a labeled expandable menu on small screens. Single-column reading at narrow widths.
## Elevation & Depth
Flat surfaces. Photography supplies depth; borders separate content. Avoid floating logo panels and decorative shadows.
## Shapes
Simple photo rectangles and lightly rounded buttons. No nested cards.
## Components
Navigation highlights the active page. Buttons describe destinations. Photos use descriptive alternatives and factual captions. Impact content separates organization-reported totals from individual program results.
## Do's and Don'ts
Use real supplied images and preserve their documentary character. Do not invent testimonials or imply sponsor amounts beyond supplied facts. Do not auto-play audio or animate numbers. Honor reduced motion and visible keyboard focus.
